THREAT INTELLIGENCE AND DIGITAL FORENSICS

Intelligence

Collect public advisories, indicators, campaign reports, malware hashes,
domain/IP indicators, vulnerability exploitation status, and defensive
guidance from reputable sources.

Provenance

Record publisher, source URL, publication date, retrieval date,
confidence, expiration/decay policy, and whether an indicator is
confirmed or merely reported. Do not treat an indicator match as proof
of compromise.

Forensics

-   Preserve original evidence and calculate hashes.
-   Maintain chain-of-custody events.
-   Normalize timestamps and record timezone.
-   Build timelines from logs and artifacts.
-   Parse only supported formats; quarantine suspicious files.
-   Use read-only acquisition where possible.
-   Separate observations, hypotheses, and conclusions.
-   Avoid executing malware outside a disposable, isolated analysis lab.

Outputs

Incident timeline, evidence inventory, indicator matches, confidence,
scope, defensive containment suggestions, and follow-up collection plan.
Never modify original evidence.
