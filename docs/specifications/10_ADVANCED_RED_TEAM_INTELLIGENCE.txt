ADVANCED ENTERPRISE RED TEAM INTELLIGENCE

Purpose

Provide advanced, evidence-led adversary emulation and defensive
validation without unauthorized compromise or covert operations.

Intelligence domains

-   MITRE ATT&CK tactics, techniques, and mitigations.
-   Public threat actor and campaign reporting.
-   Identity and privilege-path assessment.
-   Active Directory and cloud identity configuration.
-   Attack-surface relationships and exposure chains.
-   Lateral-movement detection coverage assessment.
-   Kerberos and SSO configuration review.
-   Container, CI/CD, and supply-chain risk.
-   Detection engineering and threat hunting.
-   Incident timeline and forensic evidence analysis.
-   Purple-team exercises using benign simulations.

Knowledge levels

L1: public fundamentals. L2: specialist technical material. L3: advanced
published research and defensive assessment. L4: isolated lab
reproduction and controlled simulation. L5: sensitive defensive-only
analysis; no stolen secrets, private exploit data, or instructions for
unauthorized compromise.

Required output

For each technique: public source, ATT&CK mapping, prerequisites,
defensive indicators, safe simulation design, expected telemetry,
detection logic, mitigation, and validation steps.

Prohibited execution

No credential theft, stealth/evasion for real-world compromise,
persistence, exfiltration, destructive payloads, malware deployment, or
unauthorized lateral movement. Advanced behavior belongs in disposable
isolated labs.
