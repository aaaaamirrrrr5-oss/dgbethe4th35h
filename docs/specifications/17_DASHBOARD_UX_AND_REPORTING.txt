DASHBOARD, UX AND REPORTING

UX principles

Professional, uncluttered, accessible, Android-first, Persian RTL and
English LTR support, clear status and no fake metrics. Keep dangerous
actions visually distinct and require confirmation.

Main sections

Dashboard, Projects, Authorization & Scope, Asset Inventory, Scan
Center, Test Catalog, Findings, Attack Graph, Remediation, Retesting,
Knowledge Base, Tool Registry, Threat Intelligence, Lab, Evidence Vault,
Reports, Audit Log, Backup & Recovery, Settings.

Dashboard metrics

Show only measured values: active job, progress, assets assessed, tests
executed/skipped, findings by severity/status, last knowledge update,
storage use, memory use, tool health, and recent changes. Label stale or
partial data.

Finding detail screen

Summary, severity rationale, affected asset, evidence, safe
reproduction, attack-path context, remediation, patch preview,
verification, references, history, and export.

Reports

Generate technical and executive reports in PDF/HTML/JSON where
supported. Include scope, authorization, dates, methodology,
limitations, test coverage, findings, evidence references, remediation,
and retest. Sanitize secrets and personal data.

Accessibility

Support screen readers, scalable text, keyboard navigation, high
contrast, localization, and explicit confirmation for destructive or
high-impact actions.
