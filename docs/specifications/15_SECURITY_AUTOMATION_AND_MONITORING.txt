SECURITY AUTOMATION AND CONTINUOUS MONITORING

Capabilities

-   Scheduled authorized assessments.
-   Asset and technology change detection.
-   New vulnerability/advisory matching.
-   Dependency and configuration drift alerts.
-   Finding lifecycle and remediation tracking.
-   Retest scheduling.
-   Local notifications and exportable reports.
-   Audit trail and job history.

Reliability

Use bounded queues, cancellation, retry policies, deduplication,
idempotency keys, resource budgets, and crash recovery. Do not promise
continuous monitoring while the app is suspended or device is offline.
Clearly distinguish scheduled checks from always-on monitoring.

Safety

Every recurring job inherits a validated scope and expiry. Revalidate
authorization before execution. Pause jobs when scope, certificate, DNS,
target ownership, or authorization changes. Provide emergency stop and a
visible activity indicator.

Privacy

Local-first storage. No target content upload. Export only on user
action. Redact credentials, tokens, cookies, and personal data from logs
and reports.
